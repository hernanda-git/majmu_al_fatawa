> 
> 2 - The footnotes incorporate the book Siyānat Majmū‘ al-Fatāwā min al-Saqt wa-l-Tashīf (Protecting the Collected Fatwas from Omissions and Textual Corruptions), by Shaykh Nāsir ibn Hamad al-Fahd, published by Dār Adwā’ al-Salaf, 1st edition, 1423 AH / 2003 CE.
> 
> 3 - Typographical errors and textual corruptions have been corrected, including:
> - Those identified in the earlier edition—the edition of ‘Abd al-Rahmān ibn Qāsim, may Allah have mercy on him—by the two editors of the Dār al-Wafā’ edition.
> - Those mentioned by Shaykh Nāsir ibn Hamad al-Fahd in his book Siyānat Majmū‘ al-Fatāwā min al-Saqt wa-l-Tashīf (Protecting the Collected Fatwas from Omissions and Textual Corruptions).
> - Other errors or textual corruptions.
